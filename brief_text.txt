 Sizing this honestly 
 The program is priced per hectare and we do not hold their planted area. If the external
 figure of ~8,000 t of watermelon is right, typical yields put them somewhere around
 130–200 ha — which would make the full program a six-figure annual line and our $55k
 the small end of it. Treat that as an order-of-magnitude prompt, not a number to quote at
 him. The first job on the call is simply to ask how many hectares he is planting this season,
 across melons and the rotation crops.

 How to open the call 
 He has 16 years of history with us and was written a full program two seasons ago. The opening
 should acknowledge the gap rather than pretend it isn't there. 
 Ask, in this order 
 Lead with Judah. He pointed us back to Paul — a warm door, worth naming in the first sentence. 
 The season and the area. Hectares planted, melons versus rotation crops, and whether the Alice Springs asparagus block went ahead. We quoted it in Sep 2024 and there is no order against it. 
 What happened after January 2025? Nineteen months of silence following his biggest ever order usually means something specific — a season, a price, a competitor, or a person leaving. 
 Take the program back to him. He runs Power-Cal and Opti-Cal without the Agri-Vive RF and ground-spread blend the program was built around. Agri-Vive RF at 285 L/ha is the largest volume line he is not buying. 
 Offer a fresh soil test. The most recent soil work in his folder is a 2023 copy of a 2020 test. A current test is the natural reason to re-write the program — and it is how the 2024 push started. 
 Thread — nematodes 
 A SARDI nematode diagnostic submission form sits in his Programs folder. It is a blank
 template, not a result — evidence the topic was raised, not that testing happened. If
 nematode pressure is real in those sandy Territory soils it is a strong opening for the soil
 primers (Mycro-Tec SP, Mycro-Feast) he has never bought. 
 Thread — asparagus at Alice Springs 
 Quoted 17 Sep 2024 at $2,425/ha as a dry prescription blend, 1,800 kg/ha spread rate.
 No order followed. Asparagus is a perennial with a multi-year establishment spend — if that
 block exists, it is an annuity, not a one-off. 
 What I could not establish 
 Lifetime revenue. Only the Odoo era ($55k) is quantified. Pre-2024 trading is evidenced by invoice PDFs — at least 15 invoices between 2015 and 2019 — but the values are not in a system I can total. 
 Planted hectares. Not recorded anywhere in Odoo or the drive. The single most useful number to get on the call. 
 Why they stopped. No CRM lead, no helpdesk ticket, no logged note explains the gap after Jan 2025. The record simply goes quiet. 
 Cross-checks in the agronomic brain were not possible — that index is identity-stripped by design, so grower names are removed.
